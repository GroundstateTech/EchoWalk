# Standalone operation

EchoWalk is a phone-local accessibility research prototype. Acoustic sampling, candidate distance estimation, camera display, and haptic feedback run on the Android device.

The application does not require an account, Groundstate Admin Center, organization directory, profile service, cloud API, or network connection. Authentication and multi-product identity are intentionally out of scope.

Any future optional service must preserve offline operation and must not turn loss of connectivity into loss of the core accessibility function. EchoWalk remains a research aid and not a certified mobility or life-safety device.
